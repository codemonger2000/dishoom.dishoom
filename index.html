    Pick your industry and fight your way past three villains with film trivia from 1995 to today. Every right answer is a punch. Every wrong answer, or running out of time, means your hero takes a hit.

    Level 1 needs 4 punches, level 2 needs 6, and the final boss needs 8. You have 3 lives against each villain. Lose them all and you're knocked out.

    When a villain is one punch from going down, you get a FINISHER: type the answer yourself to land the knockout.
